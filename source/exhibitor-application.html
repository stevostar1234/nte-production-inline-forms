Join us at NTE27

NTE27 returns to The Wing at Silverstone on Monday 1 March 2027, following a record-breaking event that welcomed more than 1,200 members of the Armed Forces community, over 200 exhibiting organisations and more than 100 Troops' Track Day supercars last year.

Delivered by Mission Community and Mission Motorsport, the ‘National Transition Event’ is the largest event of its kind in the UK connecting businesses, government departments and service charities along with the Armed Forces community. The event brings it all together under one roof in an expo-style format, alongside the much-loved Troops’ Track Day on the GP Circuit.

NTE creates meaningful connections, encourages collaboration and develops long lasting relationships, while providing valuable insight, shared experiences and access to opportunities.

Please note: Attendance is subject to the organisers' approval. Where payment is required, exhibitor space will only be confirmed once full payment has been received.
